MM Modern - Switch
==================
1. Copy the "switch" folder to the root of your SD card (merge it).
2. Put mm.o2r (made from YOUR Majora's Mask N64 US ROM with 2Ship 5.0.1
   "Battler Bravo" on PC) into /switch/2ship/ too.
3. Hold R while starting any game to open the homebrew menu, then open 2Ship.
